\paragraph{Exact periodic formulation.}
Let $U$ be smooth, periodic and divergence-free, let $\mathbb P$
be the periodic Leray projection, and set
\[
 \mathcal B(f,g)=\mathbb P[(f\cdot\nabla)g],\quad
 G=U_t-\Delta U+\mathcal B(U,U),\quad
 L_Uv=\Delta v-\mathcal B(U,v)-\mathcal B(v,U).
\]
For every smooth divergence-free correction $w$ the projected
unforced residual of $u=U+w$ is the \emph{exact} identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:S-unforced-expansion}
 \mathcal N(U+w)=G+w_t-L_Uw+\mathcal B(w,w).
\end{equation}
No prescribed body force has been introduced.

\paragraph{Proposition S.1 (Exact moving two-mode reconstruction).}
Let $W=\partial_zU$ and suppose $U,W$ are $L^2$-orthogonal with
nonzero norms. Write $w=\alpha U+aW+v$ and
$\langle v,U\rangle=\langle v,W\rangle=0$.
Then the full projected residual is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:S-two-mode-exact}
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathcal N(U+w)={}&
 \alpha' U+a'W+v_t-L_Uv
 +(1+\alpha)G\\
 &+\alpha\mathcal B(U,U)
 +a\,\partial_zG+\mathcal B(w,w).
\end{aligned}}
\end{equation}
The identity holds for nonconstant $U$, varying two-mode projectors
and arbitrary smooth $v$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Differentiation and bilinearity expand
$\mathcal N(U+w)$ into
$G+w_t-\Delta w+\mathcal B(U,w)+\mathcal B(w,U)+\mathcal B(w,w)$.
The coefficient of $\alpha$ satisfies
$U_t-L_UU=G+\mathcal B(U,U)$.
Because the periodic Leray projection commutes with $\partial_z$,
\[
 W_t-L_UW
 =W_t-\Delta W+\mathcal B(U,W)+\mathcal B(W,U)
 =\partial_zG.
\]
Combining these identities gives
\eqref{eq:S-two-mode-exact} without dropping the moving modes,
the time derivative, viscosity or quadratic correction.
\hfill$\square$

\paragraph{Pressure reconstruction.}
For smooth periodic divergence-free $u$ define the zero-mean
pressure uniquely by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:S-pressure-poisson}
 -\Delta p=\sum_{i,j=1}^{3}\partial_i\partial_j(u_i u_j),
 \qquad\int_{\mathbb T^3}p\,dx=0.
\end{equation}
The mean of the right-hand side vanishes, so this equation is
solvable and $\mathbb P[(u\cdot\nabla)u]
=(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p$.
Consequently $\mathcal N(u)=0$ and \eqref{eq:S-pressure-poisson}
are equivalent to the unforced physical Navier--Stokes PDE.
This reconstruction does not solve $\mathcal N(u)=0$;
it only fixes the pressure gauge for a candidate $u$.

\paragraph{Precise conditional closure statement.}
A smooth periodic divergence-free $U+w$ with a smooth pressure
from \eqref{eq:S-pressure-poisson} is an unforced Navier--Stokes
solution only if the \emph{whole vector field} in
\eqref{eq:S-two-mode-exact} vanishes at every point and time.
In addition, a finite-time singularity conclusion requires
smooth nonzero initial data, finite torus-cell kinetic energy,

Projected amplitude/translation equations alone do not establish
