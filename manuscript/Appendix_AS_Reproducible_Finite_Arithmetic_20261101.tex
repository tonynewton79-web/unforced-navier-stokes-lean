\section*{Appendix AS. Reproducible finite-arithmetic verification}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix AS. Reproducible finite-arithmetic verification}

The following self-contained source independently verifies the rational
seven-ray witnesses, the six boundary vertices of the axial--azimuthal
projection, the exact Case~006 interval, the trigonometric tensor
polynomial identity, the sharp scalar floor, and both fourfold polynomial
diagnostic integrals. The identities are checked over
\(\mathbb{Q}\) and by exact symbolic polynomial arithmetic. The positive
trigonometric polynomial integral is a kinematic diagnostic, not a
solution of the unforced equation.
