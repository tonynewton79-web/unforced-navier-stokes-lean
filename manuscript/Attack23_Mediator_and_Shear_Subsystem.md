# Attack 23: the first forced mediator and a smooth invariant-sector obstruction

**Scope.** The results below concern the exact 20-mode initial Fourier polynomial
of Attack 22 and a restricted unrotated sector. They do not establish a
sustained Navier--Stokes cascade, interaction with the shrinking singular
carrier, trace retention, or finite-time blow-up.

## 1. First omitted Fourier mode

Work on the unit three-torus with Fourier phase exp(2 pi i k·x).
Let p=(1,0,0), q=(0,2,1), r=p+q=(1,2,1), with polarizations
a=(0,1,0), b=(0,1,-2). Fix the real, solenoidal, C4-equivariant initial
20-mode orbit generated by

  uhat(p)=i A a,  uhat(q)=B b,  uhat(r)=-C b,

with A,B,C positive. Reality supplies coefficients at -k, and rotations
about the z-axis supply the remaining modes. The C4 orbit has 20 distinct
wavevectors: four from p and eight each from q,r.

At the omitted wavevector s=p+r=(2,2,1), the full 20-mode convolution
has exactly two nonzero ordered contributing pairs, (p,r) and (r,p).
With B_s = sum_{k+l=s} (uhat(k)·l) uhat(l), direct expansion gives

  B_s = (i A a·r)(-C b) + (-C b·p)(i A a)
      = -2 i A C b.

Here s·b=0, so Leray projection does not change B_s. The unforced Fourier
equation is

  d_t uhat(s) = -nu(2 pi)^2 |s|^2 uhat(s) - i(2 pi) P_s B_s.

Since uhat(s)=0 at the initial time, its derivative is

  d_t uhat(s) = -4 pi A C b != 0.

Thus the 20-mode Fourier-support ansatz is **not invariant** under
unforced Navier--Stokes whenever A C is nonzero. No conclusion about the
full carrier-plus-correction flow follows without the carrier cross terms.

## 2. The entire polarization ray

For every integer m, define k_m=(m,2,1). Then

  k_(m+1) = p+k_m,
  k_m·b = 0,
  k_m·a = 2,
  |k_m|^2 = m^2+5,
  P_(k_m)b=b.

Thus the p--k_m triadic pair has an unsuppressed transverse component b
for every m. Its viscous cost increases as nu(2 pi)^2(m^2+5).

The Lean module NonlinearMediator23.lean formalizes these rational
polarization identities and the nonzero first-mediator vector. It does
not certify full Fourier convolution or a PDE evolution.

## 3. A caution: one ray alone is an exactly smooth shear subsystem

Take x,y,z in T^3, theta=2y+z and a=e_y, b=e_y-2e_z. For arbitrary
real smooth h(t,x), g(t,x,theta), set

  u = h(t,x) a + g(t,x,2y+z) b.

This is divergence free because b·grad(theta)=2-2=0. The nonlinear
self-interaction of g vanishes and

  (u·grad)u = 2h g_theta b.

Therefore the unforced equations with spatially constant pressure hold
exactly if

  h_t = nu h_xx,
  g_t + 2h g_theta = nu(g_xx+5g_thetatheta).

For nu>0, smooth periodic data give globally smooth h and g by the heat
equation and a linear advection-diffusion equation; the latter obeys

  (1/2)d_t integral |g|^2
   = -nu integral (|g_x|^2+5|g_theta|^2).

Consequently, extending the *unrotated* triadic chain along this single
ray is not, on its own, a blow-up construction. The sum of its four C4
rotates is generally **not** confined to the same invariant subsystem,
since cross-sector interactions generate further Fourier modes.

## 4. Verification and remaining hypotheses

- A direct symbolic 20-mode convolution enumeration independently confirms
  20 unique modes, exact reality/solenoidality, and the two source pairs.
- The field-level differential identity is obtained by direct
  substitution into the 3D equation.
- The Lean file is limited to finite rational polarization algebra;
  CI/kernel status must be read from the separate GitHub Actions run.
- Unproved: complete C4 multisector closure, all mediator interactions,
  global energy and weighted norms, coupling to the frozen carrier,
  pressure effects in the complete field, and singular trace non-cancellation.

**Mathematical interpretation:** active transfer out of a finite Fourier
support is established for the isolated halo. A sustained, blow-up-capable,
unforced three-dimensional cascade remains entirely open.
